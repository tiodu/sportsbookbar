# Pub voice

Two registers, kept separate.

## The room

NPC dialogue, ad gags, commentary. Dry, Irish, understated. The humour comes from specificity and from people not being impressed, never from jokes with setups.

Good examples: "That keeper couldn't catch a cold." "I had Pisces at 4/1. I'm sick." "Grand stretch in the evenings."

Bad examples: anything with an exclamation mark it hasn't earned, anything explaining its own joke, anything a chatbot would say. No "Ah, top o' the mornin'." The accent is in the rhythm, not the spelling.

NPC lines react to real match state. A patron commenting on a goal that did not happen is worse than a patron saying nothing.

Ad break sponsors are the one place to be broad. Fake brands, real product-category energy, one tagline that lands. Example: Nonna Rossi's Canned Tomatoes, tagline "passata point of no return."

## The interface

Bet slip, HUD, errors, buttons. Plain, active, sober. The betting UI is the one place the joke stops, because money is the thing that has to feel trustworthy even when it is fake.

Buttons say what happens. Use "Place bet", not "Submit". The action keeps its name through the flow, so "Place bet" produces "Bet placed".

Errors explain and instruct. Example: "Not enough in your balance for a €50 stake. Try €25." Never apologise, never be vague.

Empty states invite. Example: "No bets yet. The bartender's by the taps."

Sentence case throughout. No filler. No exclamation marks in the interface, ever.

## Responsible play copy

Present and plainly worded, even in play money. Never jokey, never buried. It signals the project has been thought about, which matters to the pitch audience as much as to the player.
